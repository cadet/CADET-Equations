Model assumptions:
\begin{itemize}
\item the fluid density and viscosity are constant in time and space;
\item the process is isothermal;
\item the reactor is spatially homogeneous (perfectly mixed);
\item nucleation produces particles at a minimum critical size $x_{c}$;
\item the growth rate depends on supersaturation;
\item aggregation is a binary process (two particles coalesce into one);
\item the aggregation kernel is symmetric, i.e. $\beta(x, \lambda) = \beta(\lambda, x)$;
\item fragmentation follows a general daughter-size distribution (one parent particle can produce multiple fragments);
\end{itemize}


\section*{Crystallization in CSTR with nucleation and growth, aggregation, fragmentation}
The crystallization models stated in the following were established in Zhang et al. (2024) and Zhang et al. (2025).
Consider a continuous stirred tank reactor (CSTR) with volume $V(t)$, observed over a time interval $(0, T^{\mathrm{end}})$. The particle population is described by the number density $n(t, x)$ over the internal coordinate (particle size) $x\in(x_{0}, \infty)$.
The evolution of the number density is governed by the population balance equation
\begin{align}\frac{\partial (n V)}{\partial t} &= Q^{\mathrm{in}} n^{\mathrm{in}} - Q^{\mathrm{out}} n - V \left( \frac{\partial (v_{G} n)}{\partial x} - D_{g} \frac{\partial^{2} n}{\partial x^{2}} - B_{0} \delta(x - x_{c}) \right) + V \left( B_{\mathrm{agg}} - D_{\mathrm{agg}} \right) + V \left( B_{\mathrm{frag}} - D_{\mathrm{frag}} \right). \end{align}
with nucleation kinetics and regularity boundary conditions in the internal coordinate
\begin{align}
\left. \left( n v_{G} - D_{g} \frac{\partial n}{\partial x} \right) \right|_{x = x_{c}} &= B_{0}, \\
\left. \left( n v_{G} - D_{g} \frac{\partial n}{\partial x} \right) \right|_{x \to \infty} &= 0.
\end{align}
The solute mass balance is given by
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial (c V)}{\partial t} &= Q^{\mathrm{in}} c^{\mathrm{in}} - Q^{\mathrm{out}} c - \rho k_{v} V \left( B_{0} x_{c}^{3} + 3 \int_{x_{c}}^{\infty} v_{G} n \, x^{2} \, \mathrm{d}x \right).
\end{align}
Evolution of the reactor’s volume is governed by
\begin{align}
\frac{\mathrm{d} V}{\mathrm{d} t} &= Q^{\mathrm{in}} - Q^{\mathrm{out}}.
\end{align}
The constitutive relations for growth and nucleation are defined as follows. The relative supersaturation is
\begin{align}s = \frac{c - c_{\mathrm{eq}}}{c_{\mathrm{eq}}}. \end{align}
The growth rate is
\begin{align}v_{G} = k_{g} s^{g} a. \end{align}
The primary nucleation rate is
\begin{align}B_{p} = k_{p} s^{u}. \end{align}
The secondary nucleation rate is
\begin{align}B_{s} = k_{b} s^{b} M^{k}. \end{align}
where the suspension density is
\begin{align}M = k_{v} \rho \int_{0}^{\infty} n \, x^{3} \, \mathrm{d}x. \end{align}
The total nucleation rate is
\begin{align}B_{0} = B_{p} + B_{s}. \end{align}
The aggregation birth and death terms are
\begin{align}
B_{\mathrm{agg}}(x) = \frac{x^{2}}{2} \int_{x_{c}}^{x} \frac{\beta\!\left((x^{3} - \lambda^{3})^{1/3}, \lambda\right)}{(x^{3} - \lambda^{3})^{2/3}} n\!\left((x^{3} - \lambda^{3})^{1/3}\right) n(\lambda) \, \mathrm{d}\lambda, \\
D_{\mathrm{agg}}(x) = n(x) \int_{0}^{x_{\mathrm{end}}} \beta(x, \lambda) \, n(\lambda) \, \mathrm{d}\lambda.
\end{align}
The aggregation kernel is defined by a Constant kernel
\begin{align}\beta(x, \lambda) = \beta_{0}. \end{align}
The fragmentation birth and death terms are
\begin{align}
B_{\mathrm{frag}}(x) = \int_{x}^{x_{\mathrm{max}}} S(\lambda) \, b(x \mid \lambda) \, n(\lambda) \, \mathrm{d}\lambda, \\
D_{\mathrm{frag}}(x) = S(x) \, n(x).
\end{align}
The selection (fragmentation rate) function is
\begin{align}S(x) = S_{0} \, x^{3\alpha}. \end{align}
The breakage probability density function is
\begin{align}b(x \mid \lambda) = 3 x^{2} \frac{\gamma}{\lambda^{3}} \left( \frac{x^{3}}{\lambda^{3}} \right)^{\gamma - 2}. \end{align}
Here, $t$ is the time coordinate, $x$ is the particle size (internal coordinate), $n$ is the particle number density, $c$ is the solute concentration, $V$ is the reactor volume, $Q^{\mathrm{in}}$ is the volumetric inflow rate, $Q^{\mathrm{out}}$ is the volumetric outflow rate, $v_{G}$ is the growth rate, $s$ is the relative supersaturation, $c_{\mathrm{eq}}$ is the equilibrium solubility, $B_{0}$ is the total nucleation rate, $k_{g}$ is the growth rate constant, $g$ is the growth exponent, $a$ is the growth parameter, $k_{p}$ is the primary nucleation rate constant, $u$ is the primary nucleation exponent, $k_{b}$ is the secondary nucleation rate constant, $b$ is the secondary nucleation exponent, $k$ is the suspension density exponent, $M$ is the suspension density, $x_{c}$ is the critical (minimum) particle size, $D_{g}$ is the growth dispersion rate, $\beta$ is the aggregation kernel, $\beta_{0}$ is the aggregation rate constant, $S(x)$ is the selection (fragmentation rate) function, $b(x \mid \lambda)$ is the breakage probability density, $S_{0}$ is the fragmentation rate constant, $\alpha$ is the fragmentation exponent, and $\gamma_{f}$ is the daughter distribution parameter.
Consistent initial values for all solution variables are defined at $t = 0$.
