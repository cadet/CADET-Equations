General model assumptions:
\begin{itemize}
\item the fluid density and viscosity are constant in time and space (implies incompressibility);
\item the fluid flow is laminar;
\item the column is operated under constant conditions (e.g., temperature, flow rate);
\item the process is isothermal (i.e., there are no thermal effects);
\item diffusion does not depend on the concentration of the components, viscosity of the fluid, or pressure;
\item the fluid in the particles is stagnant (i.e., there is no convective flow);
\item the porous particles are spherical, rigid, and do not move;
\item the particles are homogeneous and of uniform porosity;
\item all components access the same particle volume;
\item the partial molar volumes are the same in mobile and solid phase;
\item the binding model parameters do not depend on pressure and are constant along the column;
\item the solvent is not adsorbed;
\end{itemize}


Specific model assumptions:
\begin{itemize}
\item the particles form a continuum inside the column (i.e., there is interstitial and particle volume at every point in the column);
\item the column is radially symmetric and homogeneous (i.e., concentration profiles and parameters only depend on the axial position);
\item the interstitial liquid phase concentration is spatially constant on the outer particle surface;
\item there is no surface diffusion (i.e. adsorbed molecules are spatially constant);
\item the film around the particles does not limit mass transfer. That is, we assume $k^{\mathrm{f}}_{i} = \infty$)
\end{itemize}


\section*{General Rate Model with side cavities}
Consider a cylindrical column of length $L^{\mathrm{b}} > 0$ packed with spherical particles, and observed over a time interval $(0, T^{\mathrm{end}})$.
In the interstitial volume, mass transfer is governed by the following convection-diffusion equations in $(0, T^{\mathrm{end}})\times (0, L^{\mathrm{b}})$ and for all components $i\in\{1, \dots, N^{\mathrm{c}} \}$
\begin{align}
\varepsilon^{\mathrm{b}} \frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{b}}_{i}}{\partial t} = - u \frac{\partial \left( \varepsilon^{\mathrm{b}} c^{\mathrm{b}}_{i} \right)}{\partial z} + \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \left( \varepsilon^{\mathrm{b}} D^{\mathrm{ax}}_{i} \frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{b}}_{i}}{\partial z} \right)- \left(1 - \varepsilon^{\mathrm{b}} \right) \frac{3}{R^{\mathrm{p}}} \left( d^{\mathrm{mp}} \varepsilon^{\mathrm{mp}} D^{\mathrm{p}}_{i} \left. \frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{p}}_{0,i}}{\partial r}\right)\right|_{r = R^{\mathrm{p}}},
\end{align}
with boundary conditions

\begin{alignat}{2}
u c^{\mathrm{in}}_{i} &= \left.\left( u c^{\mathrm{b}}_{i} - D^{\mathrm{ax}}_{i} \frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{b}}_{i}}{\partial z}\right)\right|_{z=0} & &\qquad\text{on }(0, T^{\mathrm{end}}),\\
               0 &= - D^{\mathrm{ax}}_{i} \left. \frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{b}}_{i}}{\partial z} \right|_{z=L^{\mathrm{b}}} & &\qquad\text{on }(0, T^{\mathrm{end}}).
\end{alignat}
Here, $t\in (0, T^{\mathrm{end}})$ is the time coordinate, $z\in (0, L^{\mathrm{b}})$ is the axial cylinder coordinate, $R^{\mathrm{p}}> 0$ is the particle radius, $c^{\mathrm{b}}_{i}\colon (0, T^{\mathrm{end}})\times (0, L^{\mathrm{b}}) \mapsto \mathbb{R}$ is the bulk liquid concentration, $\varepsilon^{\mathrm{b}}\in (0, 1)$ is the bulk porosity, $u> 0$ is the interstitial velocity, and $D^{\mathrm{ax}}_{i}\geq 0$ is the axial dispersion coefficient.
In the particles, mass transfer is governed by diffusion-reaction equations in $(0, T^{\mathrm{end}}) \times (0, L^{\mathrm{b}})\times (0, R^{\mathrm{p}})$ and for all components

\begin{align}
\frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{p}}_{0,i}}{\partial t} &= \frac{1}{r^{2}} \frac{\partial }{\partial r} \left( r^{2} D^{\mathrm{p}}_{i} \frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{p}}_{0,i}}{\partial r} \right) - \frac{1 - \varepsilon^{\mathrm{mp}}}{\varepsilon^{\mathrm{mp}}}f^{\mathrm{bind}}_{0,i}\left( \vec{c}^{\mathrm{p}}_{0}, \vec{c}^{\mathrm{s}}_{0} \right) - \sum_{m=1}^{N^{\mathrm{sc}}} \frac{d^{\mathrm{sc}}_{m} \varepsilon^{\mathrm{sc}}_{m}}{d^{\mathrm{mp}} \varepsilon^{\mathrm{mp}}} k^{\mathrm{sc}}_{m,i} \left( c^{\mathrm{p}}_{0,i} - c^{\mathrm{p}}_{m,i} \right), \\
\frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{s}}_{0,i}}{\partial t} &= f^{\mathrm{bind}}_{0,i}\left( \vec{c}^{\mathrm{p}}_{0}, \vec{c}^{\mathrm{s}}_{0} \right), \\
\frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{p}}_{1,i}}{\partial t} &= k^{\mathrm{sc}}_{1,i} \left( c^{\mathrm{p}}_{0,i} - c^{\mathrm{p}}_{1,i} \right) - \frac{1 - \varepsilon^{\mathrm{sc}}_{1}}{\varepsilon^{\mathrm{sc}}_{1}} f^{\mathrm{bind}}_{1,i}\left( \vec{c}^{\mathrm{p}}_{1}, \vec{c}^{\mathrm{s}}_{1} \right), \\
\frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{s}}_{1,i}}{\partial t} &= f^{\mathrm{bind}}_{1,i}\left( \vec{c}^{\mathrm{p}}_{1}, \vec{c}^{\mathrm{s}}_{1} \right), \\
\frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{p}}_{2,i}}{\partial t} &= k^{\mathrm{sc}}_{2,i} \left( c^{\mathrm{p}}_{0,i} - c^{\mathrm{p}}_{2,i} \right) - \frac{1 - \varepsilon^{\mathrm{sc}}_{2}}{\varepsilon^{\mathrm{sc}}_{2}} f^{\mathrm{bind}}_{2,i}\left( \vec{c}^{\mathrm{p}}_{2}, \vec{c}^{\mathrm{s}}_{2} \right), \\
\frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{s}}_{2,i}}{\partial t} &= f^{\mathrm{bind}}_{2,i}\left( \vec{c}^{\mathrm{p}}_{2}, \vec{c}^{\mathrm{s}}_{2} \right).
\end{align}

with boundary conditions

\begin{align}
- \varepsilon^{\mathrm{mp}} \left. \left( D^{\mathrm{p}}_{i} \frac{\partial c^{\mathrm{p}}_{0,i}}{\partial r} \right) \right|_{r=0} &= 0, \\
\left. c^{\mathrm{p}}_{0,i} \right|_{r = R^{\mathrm{p}}} &= c^{\mathrm{b}}_{i}.
\end{align}

Here, $r$ is the radial particle coordinate, $N^{\mathrm{sc}}\geq 1$ is the number of side-cavity types, $c^{\mathrm{p}}_{i}\colon (0, T^{\mathrm{end}}) \times (0, L^{\mathrm{b}})\times (0, R^{\mathrm{p}}) \mapsto \mathbb{R}$ is the particle liquid concentration, $c^{\mathrm{s}}_{i}\colon (0, T^{\mathrm{end}}) \times (0, L^{\mathrm{b}})\times (0, R^{\mathrm{p}}) \mapsto \mathbb{R}$ is the particle solid concentration, $\varepsilon^{\mathrm{mp}}\in (0, 1)$ is the main pore network porosity, $\varepsilon^{\mathrm{sc}}_{m}\in (0, 1)$ is the side-cavity porosity, $d^{\mathrm{mp}}\in (0, 1)$ is the main pore network volume fraction, $d^{\mathrm{sc}}_{m}\in (0, 1), \; d^{\mathrm{mp}} + \sum_{m=1}^{N^{\mathrm{sc}}} d^{\mathrm{sc}}_{m} = 1$ is the side-cavity volume fraction, $D^{\mathrm{p}}_{i}> 0$ is the particle diffusion coefficient, $k^{\mathrm{sc}}_{m,i}\geq 0$ is the cavity exchange coefficient, $f^{\mathrm{bind}}_{i}$ is the adsorption isotherm function, $\vec{c}^{\mathrm{p}}$ is the particle liquid components vector, and $\vec{c}^{\mathrm{s}}$ is the particle solid components vector.
Consistent initial values for all solution variables (concentrations) are defined at $t = 0$.
